QUOTA EXHAUSTION
----------------

After ~20 successful calls the API returned:

  ClientError: 429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED
  'You exceeded your current quota, please check your plan and billing details.'

Three probes spread over ~70 seconds were all rejected, establishing that the
cap is daily rather than per-minute. Per Google's documentation, requests-per-day
quotas reset at midnight Pacific time.

See report §6.5 for the resulting call-budget analysis.
